# IP And Legal Guidelines

## Working Assumption

Until rights are obtained, this project should be treated as an original spiritual successor or clean-room remake.

## Do Not Commit

- Original game executable files.
- Original game assets.
- Extracted textures, models, animations, sounds, music, maps, scripts, or data tables.
- Original trademarks or logos.
- Copied manual text, dialogue, mission text, or UI text.
- Screenshots used as production assets.

## Acceptable Reference Use

High-level study is acceptable for planning:

- Genre conventions.
- Publicly observable gameplay loops.
- Broad UI patterns.
- Historical themes.
- General RTS mechanics.

## Naming

Use temporary internal names until a final original title is chosen. Avoid names that are confusingly similar to protected titles, subtitles, studios, or brands.

## Clean-Room Practice

- Write original code.
- Author original data.
- Make original art and audio.
- Summarize research in original words.
- Keep references separate from production assets.

## Licensing Checklist

Before shipping anything publicly:

- Confirm title and branding clearance.
- Confirm all assets are original, licensed, or public domain with compatible terms.
- Confirm fonts and audio licenses.
- Confirm engine and plugin license obligations.
- Confirm third-party library license obligations.
