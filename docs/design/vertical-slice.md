# Vertical Slice Plan

## Objective

Create a small, complete RTS match that proves the core gameplay loop and technical direction.

## Slice Content

### Map

- Small 1v1 test map.
- Mixed open terrain and choke points.
- Two starting bases.
- Two resource types.
- At least one neutral obstacle or tactical point.

### Faction

One placeholder faction with final-quality design intent but temporary content names.

### Units

- Worker: gathers resources, constructs buildings, repairs structures.
- Ranged Fighter: basic ranged combat unit.
- Mounted or Melee Fighter: faster tactical unit or close-combat unit.

### Buildings

- Command Center: trains workers and stores resources.
- Barracks: trains military units.
- Resource Depot: nearby drop-off point.

### Resources

- Food or Supplies: gathered from map sources.
- Wood or Materials: gathered from map sources.

## Required Gameplay Systems

- RTS camera with pan, zoom, and edge scrolling.
- Unit selection and box selection.
- Right-click movement and attack commands.
- Basic group movement.
- Worker gathering loop.
- Building placement preview and construction.
- Unit training queues.
- Health, damage, attack range, cooldowns, and death.
- Simple enemy AI that gathers, builds, trains, and attacks.
- Victory and defeat conditions.

## Required Presentation Systems

- Team color support.
- Health bars.
- Selection rings or outlines.
- Basic command panel.
- Unit and building tooltips.
- Minimap placeholder.
- Basic terrain, water, foliage, dust, smoke, and impact effects.

## Quality Bar

The vertical slice is successful when a player can complete a short skirmish match and understand what is happening without developer explanation.

## Non-Goals

- Full campaign.
- Full faction roster.
- Multiplayer.
- Final art for all units.
- Complex diplomacy.
- Advanced economy chains.
- Perfect historical simulation.
