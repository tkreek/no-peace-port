# Research Plan

## Questions To Answer

- What made the historical reference game's faction design distinct?
- What parts of its economy and combat still feel worth preserving?
- Which controls feel dated by modern RTS standards?
- What unit counts should the remake support?
- What visual fidelity target is realistic for a small team?
- What pathfinding approach best supports group RTS movement?

## Reference Categories

- Classic RTS control schemes.
- Frontier, settlement, and territorial expansion themes.
- Historical clothing, buildings, tools, weapons, and landscapes.
- Modern RTS UI and accessibility standards.
- Large-unit animation optimization.

## Output Format

Research notes should capture:

- Source or reference name.
- Date reviewed.
- What was learned.
- Whether it affects design, art, tech, or production.
- Any legal concerns.
