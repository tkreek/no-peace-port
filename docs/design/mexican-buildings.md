# Mexican Building Catalog

Reference: `assets/reference/manuals/AMERICA_Manual_Win_EN.pdf`, section "Mexican structures".

These are the Mexican buildings from the manual, translated into English-facing prototype names. Costs and roles are captured as prototype data so we can gradually map each one into gameplay.

| Building | Cost | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Command Post | 500 wood | Main command building; produces comandante, women, and field workers; residence for 12 units. |
| House | 150 wood | Population space. |
| Hacienda | 300 wood | Produces gauchos, militia, cattle, and horses. |
| Cantina | 300 wood, 200 gold | Produces gunslinger; robbery/stealing upgrades. |
| Butcher Shop | 350 wood | Animal processing. |
| Barracks | 250 wood, 250 gold | Produces infantry and cavalry. |
| Gold Warehouse | 150 wood | Gold drop-off/storage. |
| Finca | 250 wood | Food storage and field/garden support. |
| Field | 150 wood | Food production; shown as a Finca child in the Mexican technology tree. |
| Sawmill | 300 wood | Wood drop-off/storage and transport wagon production. |
| Trading Post | 300 wood | Buy/sell wood, food, and rifles. |
| Weapons Factory | 500 wood, 300 gold | Produces cannons and guns. |
| Wall | 25 wood | Passive defense. |
| Tower | 200 wood | Active defense and garrison. |
| Wharf | 250 wood | Produces riverboats. |
| Church | 350 wood, 200 gold | Produces nun and priest. |
| Mission | 400 wood | Generates gold through donations; supports religious upgrades. |
| Fort | 800 wood, 500 gold | Strong active defense and garrison. |

## Prototype Asset Direction

Each building currently uses procedural Godot placeholder geometry with a custom silhouette:

- Command Post buildings: broad timber structures with labels.
- Houses: compact timber cabin.
- Hacienda/Finca: warm plaster/timber economy buildings.
- Field: low crop rows for Mexican food production.
- Cantina: darker social/commercial building with sign-like roof block.
- Butcher Shop: red-brown processing shed.
- Barracks/Fort/Tower/Wall: heavier defensive forms.
- Gold Warehouse/Sawmill: resource-facing industrial shapes.
- Trading Post/Church/Mission/Wharf: distinctive landmark silhouettes.
- Weapons Factory: forge/chimney form with cannon production.
